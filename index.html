                      Help the scanner learn

                      Every card you add or correct is kept on this phone as a training example (picture + right answer). Nothing is uploaded.

                      Backup & export

                      Your collection, wishlist, decks, sets and friends are only on this phone. Save a backup now and then – for a new phone, or if the browser data gets cleared.

                      The sell list has one row per version with its Cardmarket product id, quantity, language and condition – the format seller tools like TCG PowerTools import.

                      Data

                      Prices: Cardmarket price guide (EUR), updated daily. Card data: YGOJSON and YGOPRODeck. Prices are reference values, not live offers. Not affiliated with Konami or Cardmarket.
